User Guide

How to add products:

1. Go to "Products" tab
2. Fill in Name, Price, Stock, and Description
3. Click "Choose File" to select a picture from your phone/computer
4. Click "Add Product"
5. It will instantly appear on the main website!

How to delete products:

Scroll down to the "Manage Products" list and click the red "Delete" button.

How to update contacts:

Go to the "Contact Info" tab, change the details, and click Save.
